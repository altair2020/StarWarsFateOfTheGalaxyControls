Field notes
  • ★ marks the controls you'll use most in a fight.
  • Cycle Primary and Cycle Secondary use Alt combos by default. R and C are free on every tab captured here, so they're good single-key replacements.
  • The stick hat bindings look crossed: View Rear is on Hat Left and View Left is on Hat Right. Test them in flight before you rely on them.
  • Gamepad triggers (LT/RT) can only bind to "axis" controls for now.
  • This sheet comes from the default preset screens. Each tab's "More..." page has extra bindings not listed here.
